What you can do

Everything below runs on the device. Optional analytics on Android help improve the product; your shop records remain local.

Products & batches

Add products, restock by batch with total buying price and quantity, track selling price and expiry.

Point of sale

Search stock, build a cart, take cash or deni, enforce available quantity per batch.

Profit & dashboard

See revenue, profit from unit cost vs selling price, open deni, losses, top sellers, and low stock.

Customers & deni

Save customers, record credit sales, and log debt repayments against outstanding balances.

Low stock alerts

Android notifications when items fall to or below your threshold so you restock in time.

Sales history

Browse invoices by period, open details, and reverse a sale when you need to correct a mistake.

FAQ

Common questions about the MVP.

Does DukaLoop need internet?

No for core use. Sales, stock, customers, and reports run offline. Internet is only needed if you use optional services such as analytics event upload on Android.

Where is my data stored?

On the device database. Uninstalling the app removes local data unless you export a backup (backup tools can expand in later versions).

How is profit calculated?

At restock you enter total buying price and quantity; the app stores unit cost. Profit uses selling price minus unit cost for items sold.

What is deni?

Credit sales. If the customer pays less than the total, you attach a customer and track the balance until repayment.

Is this on Google Play?

The MVP is distributed via GitHub Releases as a sideloaded APK. Play Store distribution can follow once a publisher account and listing are ready.
